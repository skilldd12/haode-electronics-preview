Scheduled supply

In volume projects the difficulty is rarely the first shipment but the second and third. Scheduled delivery works when packing format, label content and shipping documents are fixed, so each arrival does not restart the conversation.

Process

01

Choose packing by device type: ESD bag, moisture barrier or full reel

02

Fix the label content and the document template

03

Schedule deliveries against line takt and confirm arrival each time

04

Keep goods-in records per lot for traceability

Deliverables

  • Packing and label specification
  • Staged delivery plan
  • Goods-in record per lot
