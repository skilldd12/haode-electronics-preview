About HAODE

HAODE trades chips and electronic components. This site covers that work: sourcing, brand supply and BOM kitting for purchasing, engineering and production teams at home and abroad.

ELECTRONIC COMPONENT TRADE & SOURCING

HAODE

Part verification, BOM kitting and delivery coordination.

How we work

We start with the BOM and project schedule. Alongside parameters, we align the package, lot requirements, packing format and purchasing needs for subsequent builds.

The sourcing team checks supply channels part by part, writes down what can be confirmed and leaves what cannot to the engineer. A quotation reply usually states the channel we can offer, the lot and packing, and the technical boundaries that need customer confirmation.

The catalogue provides parameters and manufacturer documentation for purchasing and engineering checks. Submit unlisted parts with a BOM or requirements list for manual sourcing.
