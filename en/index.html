Frequently asked

About the database, brand scope and how an enquiry is handled.

Can I order directly from the database?

No. The database is a selection and checking tool and shows neither price nor stock. Every part goes through an enquiry so the sourcing team can confirm channel, lot and timing before replying.

Why is there no stock count or lead time on the page?

Stock and lead time change by the hour. Printed on a page they go stale immediately, so they live in the quotation reply with the time of the check stated.

Are you a manufacturer-authorised distributor?

The directory describes sourcing scope. The supply channel and available documents for a specific part are stated in the reply for review against your purchasing requirements.

Is a base part number enough?

Yes, but say that the board marking governs or send a marking photo. Different package or temperature grade means a different order code, and checking it early saves rework.

Which attachment formats are accepted?

PDF, Excel, CSV, ZIP and common image formats, one file up to 10 MB. Uploads are used only to check the enquiry and are never exposed as public files.

What should accompany a BOM?

Include build quantity, required delivery date, whether alternatives are accepted, and any lot, packing or document requirements. List open questions in the notes if some details are still pending.

Send us the part numbers

One line or a full BOM. Attachments accept PDF, Excel and images. Every submission returns a reference number.
