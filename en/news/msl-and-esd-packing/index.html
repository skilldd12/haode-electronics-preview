Moisture-sensitive parts and ESD packing: what to check on arrival

Published 2026-09-11 Author HAODE 技术资料组

Two problems, two controls

MSL is about popcorning during reflow, controlled with dry packing and baking. ESD is about junction damage, controlled with conductive or dissipative packing and grounding. The materials differ and cannot substitute for one another.

Check the bag, then the label

Is the moisture barrier bag still under vacuum, has the humidity indicator card changed colour, are the MSL label and date code present. A breached bag means a bake before use even if the parts look fine.

Record the exposure clock

Floor humidity and exposure time together decide whether a bake is needed. Without a recorded open time, the decision is left to luck.

Judge ESD packing by resistance, not by colour

Pink and silver can both be compliant; what matters is surface resistance inside the dissipative range. Shielding bags, conductive foam and plain polyethylene are not distinguished by appearance.

Say the same on the way out

A packing note that states bag type, desiccant, humidity card and tray format is worth far more than the word professional.
