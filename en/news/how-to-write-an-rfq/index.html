How to write an RFQ that comes back quickly

Published 2026-08-29 Author HAODE 技术资料组

One: the full order code

Base plus suffix, not the family name. A marking photo helps, especially on older designs.

Two: quantity and splits

State the total and whether partial deliveries help. Pricing one piece and pricing a hundred thousand are different exercises.

Three: the substitution boundary

Say explicitly whether an equivalent part is acceptable or the exact manufacturer part is required. If it is left open, the supplier has to assume the most conservative reading.

Four: the date that matters

Say when it has to arrive, not as soon as possible. On a scheduled line, the date outranks the price.

Five: who receives and who pays

Delivery address, receiving contact, invoicing requirements, and whether datasheets or compliance documents must ship with the goods. Domestic and cross-border orders differ here.

A BOM beats a list in the email body

For multi-part enquiries, attach the BOM with must-have and optional rows marked. It is far more reliable than twenty part numbers in prose.
