Planning passive inventory when MLCC lead times move

Published 2026-09-24 Author HAODE 技术资料组

Count specifications before counting volume

A single board often carries a dozen capacitance and voltage combinations, most of which one position can cover. Merging 0402 with 0603 and 16 V with 50 V typically removes a third of the distinct items that need to be stocked.

Separate critical positions from commodity ones

Critical positions are those where a swap touches performance or qualification: filters, timing, RF matching. Commodity positions are decoupling and pull-ups. Lock the first per project, buy the second on a quarterly cycle.

Use two approved sources per position

Keep a primary and a parameter-equivalent secondary, both validated. A shortage then becomes an internal change rather than a new qualification.

Keep prototype and volume packing apart

Small reels for prototypes, full reels for volume. Mixing them produces the classic situation where stock exists but the line cannot use it.

Write down the decisions and their dates

Through a lead time that moves from four weeks to twenty, every purchasing decision should leave a trace. Next time, that timeline beats any forecast model.
