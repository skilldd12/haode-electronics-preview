Quality and delivery process

From requirements to dispatch, check the part, lot, packing and supporting records. Document scope and additional testing are agreed per part and order.

Four checks before dispatch

01

Scope

Part number, quantity, timing and substitution boundary settled in one pass, so the quote matches the requirement.

02

Channel check

Order code, lot and packing verified against manufacturer and authorised distribution before availability is confirmed.

03

Goods-in check

Marking, package, quantity and packing integrity matched against the order; exceptions recorded separately.

04

Packing and dispatch

ESD bag, moisture barrier or full reel chosen by device type, with documents travelling alongside.

Documents that travel with the goods

Datasheets

Manufacturer datasheet links or files per part. Parameters are never rewritten by us.

Lot and packing notes

Quotation and shipping documents state lot information, packing format and minimum pack quantity.

Packing list

A packing list travels with the goods for goods-in counting.

Compliance documents

Public declarations such as RoHS are provided on request; third-party test reports need separate confirmation.

Documents are agreed per part and order. Include the test scope and standard in your enquiry if third-party testing is required.

Points to agree per order

Part and substitution

Agree the full order code, package and grade. Customer engineering confirms parameter differences and validation for alternatives.

Lot and delivery

Agree lot, packing, quantity and staged deliveries. Timing follows the confirmed supply plan.

Documents and testing

Agree the document list. Additional test scope, standards, reports and costs are arranged separately.

List document requirements in the enquiry so their scope and delivery format can be confirmed individually.
